# Uco Ocu: Brand Kit

**One-liner:** Two highly educated friends who disagree about everything, and secretly need each other.

**Format:** Short videos (Shorts / Reels / TikTok, ~30–60 s) where Uco and Ocu banter about any topic:
science, politics, food, academics, everyday absurdities, the news. Born as a comic idea.

## The two personas

| | **Uco** | **Ocu** |
|---|---|---|
| Energy | Direct, loud, vocal | Calm, chill, deadpan |
| Outlook | Optimistic, sometimes *too* optimistic | Pessimistic, spirals into the worst-of-the-worst case |
| Weakness | Can't control his anger at stupidity / imbecility | Overthinks every possible disaster |
| Style | Hot takes, rants, exclamation marks | Quiet counterpoints from a completely different angle |
| Background | Highly educated | Highly educated |

**The heart of the brand:** in the end each knows the other's view matters and that his own isn't the only
right one. They never admit it out loud (ego); they keep it to themselves and stick around each other.

## Episode structure (the beats every script follows)

1. **Hook (0–3 s):** the topic + Uco's hot take (or Ocu's doom line), the strongest line first.
2. **Counter:** the other one answers from a completely different angle.
3. **Escalation (2–3 exchanges):** real facts used as ammunition; the jokes come from the clash of worldviews.
4. **The enlightening bit:** one genuinely useful fact or insight the viewer learns.
5. **The unspoken concession:** each privately acknowledges the other (a look, an aside, a thought bubble), never out loud.
6. **Button:** a final punchline or callback.

## Tone rules

- Funny, educated banter and sarcasm that **enlightens**: the viewer should leave smarter.
- Sarcasm targets **ideas, stupidity and situations**, never groups of people (ethnicity, religion, gender, etc.).
- **Politics:** critique policies, decisions and logic, not personal attacks. Keep it platform-safe.
- **Facts must be accurate.** Every factual claim in a script is checkable; sources go in the caption when useful.
- Neither character "wins". The balance is the brand.

## Running gags (use, don't overuse)

- Uco's **anger meter** rising as the topic gets dumber.
- Ocu's **worst-case-scenario spiral** ("…and then the sun explodes").
- The **almost-compliment** that gets swallowed at the last second.
